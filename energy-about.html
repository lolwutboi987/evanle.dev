The Mission

The Interactive Energy Map (evanle.dev/energy-map.html) is a dynamic platform designed to visualize regional energy distribution and its environmental impacts. By comparing fossil fuels against renewable energy sources—the largest contributors to global emissions—this project provides a critical first step toward environmental literacy and systemic infrastructure change.

Key Features

  • Geospatial Analytics: Toggle between validated data layers to observe the geographic relationships between energy plants and environmental footprints.
  • City Comparison: Select and compare municipalities to contrast their reliance on fossil fuels versus renewables and local carbon output.
  • Interactive Quiz: Engage with a gamified literacy tool that reinforces energy concepts and regional grid facts.
  • Pathways Forward: Explore the impact of grid modernization, smart microgrids, and localized community solar initiatives.

Data & Methodology

Traditional climate data is often dry and inaccessible. This tool translates complex spatial and statistical data into an intuitive visual format for students, community members, and the general public.

To ensure scientific soundness, all data is aggregated from official, peer-reviewed repositories, including the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), the National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL), and the EPA Power Profiler.
