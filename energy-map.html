Understanding the Data

This map shows how much energy we use in our homes and businesses. We track three main things:

  • Electricity: Measured in Megawatt-hours (MWh). One MWh is enough to power an average home for about a month!
  • Natural Gas: Measured in Million Cubic Feet (MMCF). We use this mostly for heating and cooking.
  • Fuel (Gasoline/Diesel): Measured in Barrels. This is the fuel that makes our cars and trucks go.

What is "Per Capita"?
To compare a big city like San Jose with a smaller one like Eureka fairly, we use "Per Capita" metrics. This means we take the total energy used and divide it by the number of people living there. We show this as the amount used per individual resident so you can see the average personal impact.

Data Sources: Electricity and Gas data comes from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA). Population numbers come from the U.S. Census Bureau. City-specific data for the West Coast represents typical usage patterns found in PG&E service areas.
