Data Methodology: This dashboard visualizes high-fidelity energy consumption patterns. State-level metrics are sourced directly from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA). City-level insights are modeled using Census ACS demographic variables (Heating Fuel, Household Income) and population-weighted indexing from the U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb API, providing statistically weighted consumption variance across urban contours.
