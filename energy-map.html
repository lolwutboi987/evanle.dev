Data Sources & Methodology

Electricity & Natural Gas: Sourced from U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) Open Data API v2. Population: Retrieved from U.S. Census Bureau 2021 Population Estimates Program. City Data: High-resolution city metrics (SF, LA, SJ, SD) are provided via PG&E aggregated community data feeds (simulated for demo).

Click any state or city marker on the map to update the detailed trend analysis and regional comparisons.
