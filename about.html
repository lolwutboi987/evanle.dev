About / Evan Le

I like the part where an idea has to survive contact with hardware.

My work moves between CAD, fabrication, physics, and the fast iteration that competitive robotics demands.

01 / Current work

Team 1516A, Panda, and Anvil.

I am the 2025–2027 Captain and Lead Designer of Robotics Team 1516A. I help set technical direction and move mechanisms from first CAD layout to tested hardware.

Outside the team, I work on open-source machines including the Panda printer platform and Anvil toolhead. I care most about packaging, iteration, and learning which assumptions fail once a design becomes physical.

What I actually work on.
