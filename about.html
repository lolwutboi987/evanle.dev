about me

I'm Evan. I design robots, build printers, and spend a lot of time turning CAD into parts. I'm the 2025-2027 Captain and Lead Designer of Team 1516A, where I take mechanisms from early layouts to tested hardware.

Outside the team, I work on the Panda printer platform and Anvil toolhead. I like figuring out how things fit together, making a prototype, and seeing what needs to change.
